An estate is a fenced plot of land that belongs to one player. The owner raises a house on it piece by piece, furnishes it, and grows herbs in its garden. It is the only land in the world a player can own.

Everything you build trains Carpentry, a skill added by this server. The garden trains Herblaw, and the bricks and nails a house needs train Crafting and Smithing.

A plot is yours for good: there is no rent, and it is never taken back. Your house stands in the open world, so everyone walking past can see it.

Quick start

Your first afternoon as a landowner, in order:

  1. Save 100,000 coins and talk to the Estate agent in the front hall of Varrock castle, just inside the main doors. Choose to buy the plot.
  2. From the agent, also buy a Saw (50 coins), a Rake (30) and, if you like, the gardening book (10).
  3. Get a hammer and a bucket from any general store. You will need a spade as well.
  4. Smith bronze bars into nails at an anvil (10 nails per bar, Smithing 1).
  5. Cut logs and walk to your plot, north-west of Varrock. Only you and your guests can open its gate.
  6. Use the logs on your carpentry table to saw them into planks.
  7. Click Build on the table. Build a planter bed and start your first herb (see Growing a herb).
  8. While it grows, choose House → Shack at the table and hammer the markers that appear (see How building works).

Tip The bank chest on your plot opens your own bank, so you can store materials right beside the table.

The estate agent

The Estate agent stands in the open front hall of Varrock castle, just inside the main doors, beside a sign that reads estate agent. They cannot be attacked. Talk to them to:

  • buy a plot, for 100,000 coins. One plot per player.
  • sell your plot back, for 50,000 coins (see the warning below).
  • manage your guest list, up to 10 players. You pick guests from your friends list or from players standing nearby; if neither has anyone, the agent says Add them to your friends list, or bring them here.
  • buy tools:

Warning Selling your plot wipes everything on it: the house, the furniture, the beds and the garden. No materials are refunded. The agent asks you to confirm and says what you will lose.

There is one plot in the world right now, 1 West Fields, Varrock. If it has an owner, the agent tells you there are none for sale.

Your plot

The plot is 14 × 14 tiles: a fence around a 12 × 12 yard, with open ground around the outside. It is in the fields north-west of Varrock, about 50 tiles south of the wilderness. You can walk there from Varrock West bank.

Materials

Planks

Use logs on your own carpentry table with a Saw in your inventory. Each log becomes one plank, and the saw works through every log of that kind in your inventory until you stop. Each plank grade needs a Carpentry level:

Logs→Plank110
Oak logs→Oak plank1515
Willow logs→Willow plank3022
Maple logs→Maple plank4535
Yew logs→Yew plank6050
Magic logs→Magic plank7570

All XP on this page is the base amount. The server's XP rate multiplies it.

Bricks

Use soft clay on any pottery oven (for example, the one in Barbarian Village) to fire a brick. It needs Crafting 5 and gives 8 Crafting XP per brick, working through all the soft clay in your inventory. Bricks are needed for the Cottage, the Manor and the ranges.

Nails

Use a bronze bar on an anvil and choose Nails (1 bar) from the craft items. You get 10 nails per bar at Smithing 1. Nails give no Carpentry XP themselves, but almost everything uses them.

The garden

The garden grows herbs from unidentified herbs. There are no seeds; any grimy herb is the seed. Each one grows into a random herb in half an hour, whether you are online or not, and gives you Herblaw XP when you pick it.

Growing a herb

  1. Rake the bed with a rake. An empty bed always has weeds, even straight after a harvest. Raking gives 1-2 weeds; keep them for the compost bin. If you leave the bed empty, the weeds grow back after 20 minutes.
  2. Plant any of the eleven unidentified herbs (needs Herblaw 3). An identified herb won't grow: Oh no, it's missing its roots.
  3. Water it with a bucket of water or a watering can. It does not start growing until it is watered.
  4. Weed it in the first 15 minutes (tend 1), and use a spade of compost on it in the last 15 (tend 2). Both are optional, but see Tending and yield.
  5. After 30 minutes, choose Gather on the plant to pick its herbs. They come out already identified, with the Herblaw XP you would get for identifying each one.

Tending and yield

A plant that got both tends, the weeding and the compost, is tended. A tended plant always survives, and its minimum harvest goes up by 2. An untended plant may die, and how well it does depends on your Herblaw level:

HerblawUntended survivesUntended herbsTended survivesTended herbs
1-2470%3-6100%5-6
25-4980%3-9100%5-9
50-7490%6-9100%8-9
75-9895%6-12100%8-12
9997%9-12100%11-12

The bed's own bonus (Maple, Yew, Magic) is added on top. A plant that dies gives nothing back, and the herb you planted is lost. Clear the dead plant away to use the bed again.

Tip Tend everything. Weeding is free, and compost comes from those same weeds. A new plot's bin starts with 5 compost, so even your first plants can be tended.

The compost bin

  • Put weeds in. Each weed rots into one compost after 20 minutes, one at a time, in the order they went in. This keeps going while you are offline.
  • Take compost out. Use a spade on the bin to get a spade of compost. Use it on a plant in its second half, and it becomes a plain spade again.
  • Examine the bin to see what is inside: 3 weeds rotting, 5 compost ready.
  • Capacity counts weeds and compost together. A full bin refuses more weeds, so use compost to make room.
  • The bin wears one point per scoop. A broken bin keeps its compost, but you can't take any out until you repair it with 3 planks of its wood, 6 nails and a hammer.

Build a better bin with Build → Compost bin at the table (6 planks of the bin's wood and 12 nails). The new bin replaces the old one, and the weeds and compost inside are kept.

Weeds

Weeds come from raking a bed and from weeding a young plant. They rot away 20 minutes after you pick them, online or offline, with Your weeds have rotted away. Get them into the bin before then.

Weeds can't be banked or traded, and dropping them throws them away.

How building works

  1. At the carpentry table, choose Build → House and pick a tier. The table marks out the house with building markers, little flagged stakes that only you can see.
  2. Walk around the house and use each marker, with a hammer and that piece's materials in your inventory. Each marker builds one piece: a wall section, a window, a floor, a roof row, the stairs or the door. You see it appear straight away, and get Carpentry XP for it.
  3. Examine a marker to see what it builds next and what it costs: Next here: the brick wall (6 bricks, 1 maple plank).
  4. The order matters: walls and windows before the roof over them. In the Manor, the stairs and upstairs floor come before anything upstairs. The door is always last.
  5. The house stays shut, even to you, until the door is fitted. Then it opens, and you can furnish it.

Roofs If houses look open-topped, you have Hide Roofs switched on in your settings. Switch it off to see your roof go up row by row.

Client The house is drawn by this server's own client. An out-of-date client can't draw the walls, and can't saw or hold the new items (Your client needs updating to make that.). The launcher keeps you up to date.

Upgrading

Starting the next tier knocks the old house down first, and the table warns you before it does. Your plot has no house until the new door is fitted. Your furniture is kept: each piece reappears in the matching room of the new house once it opens. A slot the new house has no room for waits for one that does.

While the house is being rebuilt, your bed respawn goes back to normal (see below).

Furniture

Once the door is fitted, choose Build → Furniture at the carpentry table. Each house has fixed spots for furniture; pick a spot, then a grade. You need the materials in your inventory and a hammer.

Training Carpentry

All Carpentry XP comes from your own plot:

  • Sawing planks: 10 to 70 XP each, depending on the wood. This is the steady, bankable part.
  • Building the house: 490 XP for a Shack, 1,620 for a Cottage, 8,145 for a Manor.
  • Furniture: 12 to 240 XP a piece.
  • Beds and bins: building and repairing them.

A suggested path:

CarpentryWhat to do
1-15Saw normal logs. Build two plank beds and the Shack, then a stool, a candlestick, the wall torch.
15-20Saw oak logs. Rebuild your beds in oak (4 harvests each), and build the oak bin at 20.
20-30Build the Cottage: start firing bricks early, since it takes 110. Furnish the kitchen at 25 with an iron range.
30-50Saw willow, then maple from 45. Rebuild beds in maple for the survival bonus. Stockpile 306 bricks and 189 maple planks.
50Build the Manor. Eight beds, the altar and the lectern.
60-99Yew and magic planks, yew and magic beds and the magic bin. Past the Manor, keeping the garden running is what uses the skill.
