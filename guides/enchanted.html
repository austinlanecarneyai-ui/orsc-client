Enchanted Jewellery

Every piece of jewellery in this world that an enchant spell can turn into something else - 39 products from 22 bases, each drawn with the inventory sprite the client actually renders. A base with two products does not offer a menu: it rolls, at even odds, so landing the one you wanted means crafting and enchanting more of them.

Tier 1

Sapphire & Opal

magic 7 · 136 xp · 1 water-rune, 1 cosmic-rune · 9 products

The cheap end of the ladder, and the busiest: two gems share it, so tier 1 alone accounts for nine of the thirty-nine products.

#1314

Ring of recoil

“An uncharged ring.”

Permanent members 900 gp

Reflects HALF the hit back at the attacker, rounded down but never less than 1 on a hit that landed - so 1 back on a hit of 1 or 2, 4 on a hit of 9, 10 on a hit of 20. A miss reflects nothing. Against NPCs only: nothing is reflected in player-versus-player. Melee, ranged and magic alike - but never a cannon, because the only cannon in the game is fired by a player and a player is not an NPC. No charge limit and it never shatters.

From Sapphire ring (craft 8) · Enchant lvl-1 amulet — an even-odds roll against Ring of rebound

#1599

Ring of rebound

“What I throw tends to come back”

Permanent 900 gp

One thrown weapon in three is not consumed - darts, knives, spears and javelins. It is not a return to the inventory: the weapon simply is not spent, which matters because with the equipment tab on it would otherwise come off the equipped stack and pile up in your pack. No charge limit and it never shatters.

From Sapphire ring (craft 8) · Enchant lvl-1 amulet — an even-odds roll against Ring of recoil

#1594

Necklace of tending

“It watches the pot for me”

Permanent 1,050 gp

A cook that would have burnt is re-rolled once. It does not stop burns outright - a cook far above your level still mostly burns - it wins back the ones that were close. Covers every cooking fire and range, and the gnome cooking bench. No charge limit and it never shatters.

From Sapphire necklace (craft 10) · Enchant lvl-1 amulet

#1604

Amulet of clarity

“My spells cost me less”

Permanent 900 gp

One cast in three consumes no runes at all - enchanting, alchemy, teleports, telegrab and superheat. Combat spells are NOT saved: the roll is made and then thrown away, and the runes are taken anyway. You must still be holding the runes: it saves them, it does not let you cast without them. No magic bonus. No charge limit and it never shatters.

From Sapphire Amulet (craft 13) · Enchant lvl-1 amulet — an even-odds roll against Sapphire Amulet of magic

#1614

Ring of Agility

“I never lose my footing”

Permanent 750 gp

No agility obstacle in the game can be failed while it is worn - the courses, the shortcuts, the Shilo Village fissures, the Legends Quest caves and crevice, and the Underground Pass ledges and rocks. It removes the failure roll and NOT the level requirement: every obstacle still refuses anybody short of the agility it asks for, and the ring is worth nothing to them. Rolls that are not agility - the Underground Pass orbs, the Tourist Trap slope, the Legends rope - are untouched, because those are fear and luck rather than footing. No charge limit and it never shatters.

From Jade ring (craft 18) · Enchant lvl-2 amulet — an even-odds roll against Ring of Dexterity

#1615

Ring of Dexterity

“My aim is steadier at range”

Permanent 750 gp

Twelve extra aim points on every ranged attack - bows, crossbows, darts, throwing knives and spears alike, and against players and monsters alike. They are real aim points in the same currency the weapon's own aim is in, so with a longbow they are worth about fifteen per cent more hits. It does nothing at all for melee or for magic. No charge limit and it never shatters.

From Jade ring (craft 18) · Enchant lvl-2 amulet — an even-odds roll against Ring of Agility
