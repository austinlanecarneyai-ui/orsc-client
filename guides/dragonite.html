Step 1Thurgo's hammer

Thurgo, the Imcando dwarf south of Port Sarim

Once you have finished Hero's Quest, Thurgo has a new line in his menu. Choose "Something else" if you just want his usual conversation.

I need a hammer strong enough to forge dragon steel Thurgo: The Imcando were master smiths... Thurgo takes a long look at your hands Can you make me a dwarven hammer? Thurgo: 100,000 coins, and one of those redberry pies of course It's a deal Thurgo hands you a dwarven hammer
  • Below 90 Smithing he calls you an apprentice and won't discuss it. He checks every time you ask.
  • The coins and the pie must be in your inventory, and the pie can't be a note. If you're short, he says exactly what's missing and takes nothing.
  • You can buy as many hammers as you like. They're tradeable.
  • Ask "How do I forge dragonite steel bars?" and he'll give you the recipe and where to find the furnace.

Step 3The Magma Furnace

In the lava cavern beneath the volcano, far north in the Wilderness (level 64 at the furnace)

Getting there. From the Wilderness, head north to the volcano:

  1. A lava field seals the south. There's a single gap through it, and an island forces a dog-leg on the way through.
  2. Pass the ruins of Kilnmouth to the west lava river. Cross it on the three stepping stones (Agility 70; a slip burns you and you carry on), or walk the long way round by Ridge Street.
  3. Wilderness Town lies beyond. The Wilderness Bar is at its heart.
  4. Climb the volcano's north-west flank through four gates to the crater, and take the ladder down.
  5. Follow the cavern to the lava ocean. The furnace is on the spit.

Smelting. Use a dragon sword or dragon axe on the furnace. It checks your level and your ingredients, and takes nothing unless everything is there.

You lower the dragon sword into the magma The magma burns the impurities out of the metal You retrieve a bar of purified dragonite

Bank at the Wilderness Bar. Its Magic chest opens your own bank, and nobody can fight inside the bar's walls. It's the nearest place to restock between furnace trips.

Step 4The Imcando anvil

The back of the Dwarven Mine, behind Brokkr's door

A normal anvil can't take dragonite. Use a bar on one and you're told the anvil would break under it.

Brokkr lets you through only if you're carrying a Dwarven Hammer. Talk to him, or just open the door. You can always walk back out, hammer or not.

Inside, use a Dragonite bar on the Imcando anvil with:

  • 10 Dragonite bars, unnoted
  • a Dwarven Hammer
  • 90 Smithing

The ten bars go, the hammer shatters, and the sword is yours.
