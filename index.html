Two things that surprise people

The wilderness AI are combat 24–46. You can only fight somebody in the wilderness when the difference in combat level is no more than the wilderness level you are both standing on, and it works both ways. The AI post up on the border road at wilderness 1–4, so a character outside about combat 20–28 will neither attack them nor be attacked. That is the game's own rule, not a bug — they are not broken, you are out of range.

“This client is out of date.” Close it and run Play-OpenRSC.cmd again. The launcher updates the folder before it starts the game, so the second run gets you in. If it keeps saying it, your machine cannot reach the update server.
