RS Classic Continuum

RuneScape Classic, preservation ruleset, with a few things added.

Download the launcher (69 MB)

Windows 10 or 11. Java comes with it, and nothing needs an administrator. After this the launcher keeps the game up to date.

Windows will warn you the first time. The launcher is not signed, so SmartScreen says “Windows protected your PC”. Click More info, then Run anyway. If your browser asks whether to keep the file, choose Keep.

Playing

RunRS-Classic-Continuum.exe, then Install. It installs for you only, in %LOCALAPPDATA%\RS Classic Continuum, with a desktop shortcut.

Playing from the old folder?

If you start the game with Play-OpenRSC.cmd, that folder no longer updates. Get the launcher above. It finds the old folder and offers to copy your controller settings. Your character is on the server, so nothing else needs moving. Delete the old folder afterwards.

Two things that surprise people

The wilderness AI are combat 24–46. You can only fight somebody in the wilderness when the difference in combat level is no more than the wilderness level you are both standing on, and it works both ways. The AI post up on the border road at wilderness 1–4, so a character outside about combat 20–28 will neither attack them nor be attacked. That is the game's own rule, not a bug — they are not broken, you are out of range.

“This client is out of date.” Close the game and press Play in the launcher again. It updates the game before it starts it.
